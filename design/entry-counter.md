# CS5 entry counter (2026-11-14)

CS5 is a second REV Color Sensor V3 named `CS5`, nearer the intake than the counter's CS1 to CS4. `subsystems/EntryCounter.java` counts the elements as they ENTER the intake from CS5's proximity, using
`subsystems/PeakCounter.java`, `subsystems/EntryGrouper.java` and `subsystems/EntryPipeline.java` (the pure pipeline the thread runs: baseline, peaks, valley rule, merge). **It counts and logs everywhere; one action so far (2026-11-14, below): in TELEOP - COMPETITION the intake goes OUT when 4 elements have entered.** The autos and the tuners do not act on it.
`Mechanisms.fromHardware` builds it (null without `CS5` in the configuration), so the TeleOps, the autos and the bring-up opmodes all get it. The CS Pass Study switches it off (it reads CS5 itself).

## Why peaks
The 2026-11-14 CS Pass Study (see `opmodes.md`) with the operator pressing B once per element fed (31 elements, the ground truth) showed: one element is a bump of +8 to +44 counts on an empty level of about 65
(noise about 0.5); a fixed threshold with hysteresis merged the elements of a clump (the dips between touching elements are only 2 to 4 counts above the empty level) and found 61 percent at 79 percent precision.
Counting each local peak with a PROMINENCE of at least 4 to 6 counts found 30 of 31 at 97 percent precision (20 ms), and 29 to 30 of 31 at 96 to 97 percent on a 30 ms grid (prominence 6, no smoothing). Clumps of 3 and 4
fed 0.3 s apart came out exactly. CS5 does not see the pusher arm (the proximity stayed at the empty level through 6 of 8 salvos), so there is no mask while the pusher moves.
`tools/entry/run-check.cmd` replays the recorded run (`tools/entry/data/`) through the Java class and checks it against the Python reference.

## What runs
- **Thread** (starts at the first `Mechanisms.update`, nothing is read in INIT; ends when the updates stop): `rawOptical()` (one 2-byte I2C read, about 3 ms) every `EntryCounter.PERIOD_MS` (**25** since 2026-11-14; 30, then 20 before: the 14:16 TeleOp at 20 ms ran 31.8 ms mean, 26 percent of the loops over 40 ms and 7 Pinpoint blips in 72 s), each sample into `PeakCounter` (`PROMINENCE` 6, `SMOOTH` 1, `MIN_SEPARATION_SEC` 0.06) and its peaks into `EntryGrouper` (`MERGE_HEIGHT` 45, `MERGE_GAP_SEC` 0.4); all Dashboard values, read when the thread starts.
  The sensor is put on its fast proximity preset (8-bit, a new value every 6.25 ms) when it is built (INIT): the SDK's default would be 10 Hz.
- **Cost** (measured 2026-11-14): burst reads that also read the colors: 10 ms = 69 percent of the hub and a 47 ms main loop against the TeleOp's 26; 20 ms = 34 percent and 40 ms. The 2-byte thread at 30 ms in the 13:57 TeleOp: the loop ran 28.9 ms mean (max 68) against 25.9 (max 137), 17 percent of the loops over 40 ms against 8 percent; each read took 6.2 to 6.7 ms of wall-clock time (queued behind the main loop's commands; 2.7 ms alone). At 20 ms expect about +4.5 ms; **raise `PERIOD_MS` if more than about 20 percent of the loops go over 40 ms.**
- **Sampling:** every 60 to 80 ms (unsmoothed) still found 94 to 97 percent of the elements in the recorded run, so 30 ms has margin; `PERIOD_MS` can be raised if the loop suffers.

## The merge rule (EntryGrouper, 2026-11-14)
The 13:57 TeleOp (the shadow counter in a real drive) counted 39 entries against 23 held at the six salvos: big excursions (+45 to +163 counts; nothing in the hand-fed study went over +44) come with bursts of sub-peaks (8 entries in 1.8 s while one element was gained). Peaks whose times are less than `MERGE_GAP_SEC` apart form a group; a group with a peak of at least `MERGE_HEIGHT` counts is counted ONCE (at its tallest peak); a group of smaller peaks is counted peak by peak, so the study's clumps of 3 and 4 elements are untouched. On the 13:57 raw peaks it gives 23; on the study run nothing changes (31). An entry is reported up to about `MERGE_GAP_SEC` + 0.2 s after its peak. It may undercount real elements that arrive close together at high amplitude (at 45.6 to 46.5 s the counter gained 2 and the rule counts 1), and a wall should probably count 0, not 1: gating by pose waits for the empty-robot drive test. `MERGE_HEIGHT` 100000 turns the merging off.

### First run (14:36 and 14:41 TeleOps, 25 ms thread)
The thread cost nothing measurable at 25 ms: the loop ran 25.8 / 25.6 ms mean (max 82 / 80) against the TeleOp's 25.9, 11 percent of the loops over 40 ms (8 percent without the thread; 17 percent at 30 ms and 26 at 20 ms), 1 to 2 Pinpoint blips per 120 to 170 s; each read 6.4 ms of wall-clock time, 26 percent of it, interval 25.0 ms with at most 28 ms. The 14:41 run (robot stationary, 11 salvos and 3 rejects with nothing in it, 121 s): 0 entries and the proximity 65 to 67 throughout, so the pusher arm, salvos and rejects do not trigger CS5. ## EDGE counting (2026-11-15, the user's idea; the robot's counter now)
The peaks (below) overcounted in real use: one element parked under CS5, its wiggles and the ringing of one rolling element each made peaks, so a load of 4 was 10 or 12 entries. The robot now counts the **RISE** of the proximity: an entry is the signal reaching `EDGE_HI` (6) counts above the empty level for `EDGE_CONFIRM` (2) samples, counted at the rise (about 50 ms later, not the 0.5 s the peaks and the merge took), and the next one needs the signal back at `EDGE_LO` (2) counts above the empty level or lower. There is no fall to detect for the count, so a persistent element is ONE entry (all its readings stay above the threshold) at the price of a clump that never dips back counting once (an undercount, accepted). A signal that stays above `EDGE_LO` for 10 s puts out `the proximity has read above the empty level ... for 10 s` (a parked element, or the empty level moved) and no more entries come until it drops. `USE_EDGE` false goes back to the peaks (`PROMINENCE`, `MERGE_*`, `VALLEY_MAX`; the studies and the replay check keep them).
- **Replayed** (`tools/entry/run-check.cmd`, the same `EntryPipeline.edge` class): the 31-element study run 31 entries, 30 of 31 presses matched; the 14:16 TeleOp 0 in the 30 s empty drive and exactly 12 in the three loads of 4; the 15:22 TeleOp 25 entries in all where the peaks gave 37 (the counter held 25 at its nine salvos). A sweep of the rise over +4 to +8 and 1 to 2 samples gave the same results with the re-arm at +2; a re-arm of +3 gave 32 on the study, and requiring the signal to stay low before re-arming cost recall (74 to 77 percent).
- **The empty level is the 90th percentile of the first second**, not the median: the empty proximity flickers between two neighbouring counts (65 and 66 in the study, 66 and 67 on other days) and with the median a 1-count difference in which of the two it landed on moved the study from 31 entries to 28. Keep the intake clear for the first second. It can only be lowered afterwards.
- **No counter in the FULL logic any more (user, 2026-11-15: the sensors on CS1 to CS4 do not see every element):** the FULL guard (`ENTRY_FULL_MIN_COUNTER`) is 0 (off), the reset on the counter reading 0 (`ENTRY_ZERO_RESET_SEC`) is 0 (off), and the lock for FULL no longer needs the counter to read anything. The count restarts at a salvo or a reject. The launched tally is the higher of the entry count and the counter's count, at most 4. The match screen's `N HELD` is the CS5 entry count since the last salvo or reject (2026-11-15, user: never the other color sensors' counts), green at 4 or when FULL; `? HELD` in yellow with `CS5 NOT ANSWERING` when CS5 is missing or silent.
- Open: the comparison on the TeleOps used the counter's "N held" at each salvo as a proxy, which can include elements left over from before; a deliberate run with a known number fed would settle it.

## The FULL sequence (2026-11-15, user: replaces the order below)
0. **The intake keeps running IN for `FULL_IN_HOLD_SEC` (0.1 s)** after FULL is declared (screen `FULL: INTAKE RUNNING ON`), so the 4th element, counted at the rise of its bump about 50 ms in, is in first: a single element is above the threshold for 80 to 120 ms (median, the study and the 14:16, 15:12 and 15:22 TeleOps; p90 about 260 to 290 ms for normal passes, longer for parked ones), so 60 to 100 ms of its pass is left after the count. CS5 sees when the element passes its view, not when it has seated in the transport.
1. **The intake runs OUT for `FULL_PRE_OUT_SEC` (0.1 s)** (screen `FULL: REVERSING`). The pusher does not move yet.
2. **The intake STOPS, and only then does the pusher move** to the lock position (`FULL: LOCKING`; the intake stays stopped while the arm moves: the lock takes about 0.3 to 0.5 s). If the lock is refused (`PUSHER LOCK for FULL refused: ...`, `FULL: LOCK REFUSED`) the intake goes back IN: a stopped intake with the arm open lets the elements roll out.
3. **Once LOCKED the intake runs OUT** (`FULL_LOCKED_INTAKE_OUT`, true; `FULL: LOCKED, INTAKE OUT`), so the entrance stays clear while the robot is full; peaks of elements leaving while it is OUT are not counted. With `FULL_LOCKED_INTAKE_OUT` false it stays stopped after the lock and runs OUT only to clear a fifth element under CS5 (the earlier design below).
A salvo (from the lock), a reject or the operator's right trigger (forces the intake IN and unlocks) ends it. Not yet run on the robot.

## The actions on FULL (2026-11-14, user)
The counter on CS1 to CS4 does not work when the track is over-full, and with pollen only and the pusher at HOLD the track holds **4**. So in `TeleOp - Competition` only (`Mechanisms.setEntryFullActionsAllowed(true)`; master switch `ENTRY_FULL_ACTIONS`, each action has its own Dashboard switch, `ENTRY_CAPACITY` 4) the robot is **FULL** when 4 elements have entered by the CS5 count since the track was last emptied. The count restarts at 0 when **a salvo starts**, when **a reject finishes**, or when **the counter on CS1 to CS4 has read 0 for 3 s** (a new entry restarts those 3 s). CS5 can only declare FULL while the counter holds at least 3 (`ENTRY_FULL_MIN_COUNTER`, 2 before the 15:12 run; 0 turns it off). While FULL:
- **The intake is STOPPED once the pusher is LOCKED** (`FULL_STOPS_INTAKE`; the first version ran it OUT; stopped before the arm was across the entrance, the elements roll back out, as in the 15:12 run). The operator holding the right trigger overrides it (a wrong FULL must never block collecting).
- **A fifth element under CS5 sends the intake OUT** (`FULL_CLEARS_FIFTH`), only while FULL and LOCKED, and only once the watch is ARMED: the sensor must first read within `FIFTH_CLEAR_COUNTS` of empty for `FIFTH_ARM_EMPTY_SEC` (0.5 s) since FULL (an element resting under CS5 after FULL is one of the four, not a fifth). Then the proximity staying `FIFTH_DWELL_COUNTS` (8; 5 in the first version) above empty for `FIFTH_DWELL_SEC` (0.3 s; 0.2). An entry counted while FULL no longer triggers it (the first version did: the resting element rings). It runs OUT for at least `FIFTH_MIN_OUT_SEC` (0.6 s) until the proximity has been within `FIFTH_CLEAR_COUNTS` (3) of empty for 0.3 s, then stays stopped; it gives up after `FIFTH_CLEAR_MAX_SEC` (3 s) and waits `FIFTH_RETRY_SEC` (2 s). Screen: `FULL: CLEARING 5TH`; log: `ENTRY FIFTH ...`.
- **The pusher LOCKS** across the entrance (`FULL_LOCKS_PUSHER`, the same arm lock as before but requested by FULL, and it now goes forward from WHEREVER THE ARM RESTS (after a salvo it rests 40 to 60 deg past a HOLD until a pusher check parks it, and with the intake always IN the check is skipped; the first version needed the arm at HOLD and never locked); a refusal is logged `PUSHER LOCK for FULL refused: ...`; `PUSHER_LOCK_ENABLED`, off since 11-06, still gates the target-lock and Hive Dash locks). A salvo goes from the lock (310 deg), a reject unlocks first, the operator's right trigger unlocks it, and it goes back to HOLD when FULL ends without a launch.
- **The auto-launch is ARMED** (`TeleOpCompetition.AUTO_LAUNCH_WHEN_FULL`), like the operator's right bumper: inside the launch zone of the locked cell the chassis stops and a salvo goes when the lock is aligned and the robot has stopped (the driver still holds the target lock). Screen: `AUTO-LAUNCH ARMED`; log: `AUTO LAUNCH armed by FULL`.
- The screen shows `FULL: INTAKE STOPPED` (or `CLEARING 5TH`).
Not changed: the autos and the Launch Tuners; the operator's left trigger (OUT) and the reject.

**The launched tally:** at each salvo start the elements launched are the counter's count when it reads (the entry count when it does not, at most 4; the entry count ran 3, 10, 6 and 11 at four salvos the counter saw as 4, 4, 4 and 3); `SALVO started: ... | LAUNCHED n (the entry count a, the counter b): total in all`; the screen shows `N SHOT` (small, under the pose); sample column `launched_total`; the verbose screen `Launched (elements)`.
Peaks of elements leaving while the intake is OUT are not counted: an entry is judged by the time of its PEAK against the stretches the intake was OUT (plus 0.4 s after; an entry is confirmed up to 1.5 s after its peak, so judging by the time of the log line, as the first version did, counted 6 elements leaving during a reject at 14:36 / 124.9 s as entries). A new entry also restarts the 3 s of the counter's zero (the first version reset the count the instant the first element entered, because the counter had not seen it yet). New columns `cs5_held` (the count) and `cs5_full` (0 or 1) in every log.
### The 15:12 TeleOp (first run of these actions): what went wrong and what changed
- The pusher lock NEVER started (atHold was false after every salvo: the arm rests at +48 / +58 deg past a HOLD); with the intake stopped the elements rolled back toward the mouth, a roll-back showed as an element under CS5, the fifth-element rule sent the intake OUT (8 times) and pushed held elements out (the counter 4 -> 3). Fixes: the lock goes forward from the resting arm; the intake stops only once LOCKED (else it stays IN); the fifth watch is armed only after the sensor has read empty, needs +8 for 0.3 s, and has no entry trigger.
- The entry count overcounts in real use by about 1 (one element rolling past rings with peaks 0.12 to 0.15 s apart, +6 to +19, which the merge rule leaves alone because none is +45): 3 entries for a load the counter saw as 4, then 5 for 3 to 4; and it keeps counting after FULL, so the count at the salvo was 10 and 11. FULL guard raised to a counter of 3, the tally now uses the counter. Still open: whether CS5's count is good enough alone (the study's volleys were 0.15 to 0.3 s apart, the same as the ringing).
- Worked: AUTO LAUNCH armed by FULL and fired in the RED_REAR zone at 94.2 s with 4 held.
Not yet tested on the robot after the fixes: the lock on FULL, the intake stop behind it and the fifth-element clearing. A confirmation delay of up to half a second (the merge grouping) sits between the fourth element and the intake stopping; `MERGE_GAP_SEC` is the thing to shorten if a fifth slips in.

## The valley rule (EntryPipeline, 2026-11-14, from the 14:16 TeleOp): OFF by default (`VALLEY_MAX` 100000, the user's decision: the robot goes OUT when full, which removes the parked-element case, and tight groupings whose dips do not return to the empty level are expected; `VALLEY_MAX` 3 turns it on again)
The 14:16 TeleOp (3 salvos, the operator: 4 pollen in the bot at every salvo; driven EMPTY for the first 30 s; at the end a nectar and too many pollen picked up and the robot stopped loaded): **0 entries in the 30 s of empty driving** (1080 in of path, 1621 deg of turning, raw proximity 64 to 67 throughout; it was within 6 in of a wall ahead for only 0.2 s, so walls and flowers are still not tested), **exactly 4 entries in each of the three loads** (the first 12 entries are the 12 pollen), then 20 entries at the loaded end (a nectar and pollen parked in front of the sensor: the proximity sat 6 to 7 counts above empty with wiggles of +6 to +15 on top). A real element's bump comes back DOWN to the empty level before the next (the valley before each of the first 16 entries: -2 to +2 counts around the empty level; the study run: -1 to +3); the wiggles at the end had valleys +4 to +8. So a peak whose valley is more than `VALLEY_MAX` (3) counts above the empty level is REJECTED (counted in the `rejected` figure of the logs, not as an entry). The empty level is the median of the first `BASELINE_SEC` (keep the intake clear then); it can only be LOWERED afterwards (a quiet 2 s clearly lower), so an object parked in front of the sensor can never raise it. `tools/entry/run-check.cmd` replays the 14:16 proximity through the same `EntryPipeline` class: 18 entries (0 empty, 15 up to the loaded end, 3 at the end for the nectar and the pollen that arrived), 14 wiggles rejected; the study run is still 31. The cost: dense clumps whose dips stay more than 3 counts above empty are undercounted (the 10 ms study run: 47 of 60 raw peaks kept, against 52 shot by the counter's count).

## What it logs
- `CS5 ENTRY COUNTER started ...` (the settings and whether the proximity preset applied) and `CS5 ENTRY COUNTER baseline: ...` (the empty level and noise over the first second after the thread starts: **keep the intake clear for the first second of the run**).
- `CS5 ENTRY n: a peak of +h counts (prox p) at t s of the thread | <the counter's held text>` for every counted element.
- `CS5 ENTRY THREAD: ...` every 15 s: the real interval, the read time, the share of the hub, entries, errors, and `CS5 ENTRY COUNTER NOT ANSWERING` / `answering again` if it stops for 0.5 s.
- `SALVO started: ... | CS5 shadow count: n entries since the last salvo started (m in all)`: compare n with the "N held" of the same line (the counter's count).
- Sample columns `cs5_entries`, `cs5_prox` and **`cs5f`** (2026-11-14) in every log: `cs5f` is every raw proximity sample since the previous row as `prox@-ms` (ms BEFORE that row's own time, so `row t - ms` is the log-clock time of the sample), about 5 per 100 ms row at 20 ms. A merged entry's line lists its raw peaks (`MERGED n peaks: +26@15.51 ...`) and says how many ms before the line the peak was.
- the verbose TeleOp screen shows `CS5 entries (shadow, not used)`.

## FULL AUTO / COUNT ONLY (2026-11-16)

The automatic actions on a CS5 FULL are OFF by default (changed 2026-11-18, user: the choice at INIT with gamepad 1 X was removed and the default is COUNT ONLY). `TeleOpCompetition.FULL_AUTO` (false) turns them on, only in the code or on the Dashboard before Start:

- **FULL AUTO**: FULL (4 entries since the last salvo or reject) runs the intake IN 0.1 s, OUT 0.1 s, stops it, locks the pusher, runs the intake OUT while locked (or clears a fifth under CS5), and arms the auto-launch.
- **COUNT ONLY**: the count is shown on the driver screen (with a COUNT ONLY note) and logged (`ENTRY FULL ... COUNT ONLY`), nothing acts on it: the intake is always IN, no lock on FULL, no auto-launch from FULL, as at Hartland. The operator's own auto-launch toggle, the operator right trigger and the target-lock pusher lock work as before.

Both the INIT screen and the competition screen name the mode, and the log's SETUP line has it. Not switchable after Start.

## Flywheel position servo (2026-11-16)

The competition TeleOp and the autos hold the POLLEN position (`fly_pos` at 0.45), no toggle: `Mechanisms.holdFlywheelPollenAtStart()` in the TeleOp (nothing moves in INIT, G403), `Mechanisms.fromHardware` in the autos and tuners (at INIT, like the ramp), and a safety net on the first mechanisms update. A launch waits out the servo's travel after a first command. The nectar position, the indexed launch and the pusher PID are on the flywheel-servo-prototype branch.
