# CS5 entry counter (2026-11-14)

CS5 is a second REV Color Sensor V3 named `CS5`, nearer the intake than the counter's CS1 to CS4. `subsystems/EntryCounter.java` counts the elements as they ENTER the intake from CS5's proximity, using
`subsystems/PeakCounter.java`, `subsystems/EntryGrouper.java` and `subsystems/EntryPipeline.java` (the pure pipeline the thread runs: baseline, peaks, valley rule, merge). **It counts and logs everywhere; one action so far (2026-11-14, below): in TELEOP - COMPETITION the intake goes OUT when 4 elements have entered.** The autos and the tuners do not act on it.
`Mechanisms.fromHardware` builds it (null without `CS5` in the configuration), so the TeleOps, the autos and the bring-up opmodes all get it. The CS Pass Study switches it off (it reads CS5 itself).

## Why peaks
The 2026-11-14 CS Pass Study (see `opmodes.md`) with the operator pressing B once per element fed (31 elements, the ground truth) showed: one element is a bump of +8 to +44 counts on an empty level of about 65
(noise about 0.5); a fixed threshold with hysteresis merged the elements of a clump (the dips between touching elements are only 2 to 4 counts above the empty level) and found 61 percent at 79 percent precision.
Counting each local peak with a PROMINENCE of at least 4 to 6 counts found 30 of 31 at 97 percent precision (20 ms), and 29 to 30 of 31 at 96 to 97 percent on a 30 ms grid (prominence 6, no smoothing). Clumps of 3 and 4
fed 0.3 s apart came out exactly. CS5 does not see the pusher arm (the proximity stayed at the empty level through 6 of 8 salvos), so there is no mask while the pusher moves.
`tools/entry/run-check.cmd` replays the recorded run (`tools/entry/data/`) through the Java class and checks it against the Python reference.

## What runs
- **Thread** (starts at the first `Mechanisms.update`, nothing is read in INIT; ends when the updates stop): `rawOptical()` (one 2-byte I2C read, about 3 ms) every `EntryCounter.PERIOD_MS` (**25** since 2026-11-14; 30, then 20 before: the 14:16 TeleOp at 20 ms ran 31.8 ms mean, 26 percent of the loops over 40 ms and 7 Pinpoint blips in 72 s), each sample into `PeakCounter` (`PROMINENCE` 6, `SMOOTH` 1, `MIN_SEPARATION_SEC` 0.06) and its peaks into `EntryGrouper` (`MERGE_HEIGHT` 45, `MERGE_GAP_SEC` 0.4); all Dashboard values, read when the thread starts.
  The sensor is put on its fast proximity preset (8-bit, a new value every 6.25 ms) when it is built (INIT): the SDK's default would be 10 Hz.
- **Cost** (measured 2026-11-14): burst reads that also read the colors: 10 ms = 69 percent of the hub and a 47 ms main loop against the TeleOp's 26; 20 ms = 34 percent and 40 ms. The 2-byte thread at 30 ms in the 13:57 TeleOp: the loop ran 28.9 ms mean (max 68) against 25.9 (max 137), 17 percent of the loops over 40 ms against 8 percent; each read took 6.2 to 6.7 ms of wall-clock time (queued behind the main loop's commands; 2.7 ms alone). At 20 ms expect about +4.5 ms; **raise `PERIOD_MS` if more than about 20 percent of the loops go over 40 ms.**
- **Sampling:** every 60 to 80 ms (unsmoothed) still found 94 to 97 percent of the elements in the recorded run, so 30 ms has margin; `PERIOD_MS` can be raised if the loop suffers.

## The merge rule (EntryGrouper, 2026-11-14)
The 13:57 TeleOp (the shadow counter in a real drive) counted 39 entries against 23 held at the six salvos: big excursions (+45 to +163 counts; nothing in the hand-fed study went over +44) come with bursts of sub-peaks (8 entries in 1.8 s while one element was gained). Peaks whose times are less than `MERGE_GAP_SEC` apart form a group; a group with a peak of at least `MERGE_HEIGHT` counts is counted ONCE (at its tallest peak); a group of smaller peaks is counted peak by peak, so the study's clumps of 3 and 4 elements are untouched. On the 13:57 raw peaks it gives 23; on the study run nothing changes (31). An entry is reported up to about `MERGE_GAP_SEC` + 0.2 s after its peak. It may undercount real elements that arrive close together at high amplitude (at 45.6 to 46.5 s the counter gained 2 and the rule counts 1), and a wall should probably count 0, not 1: gating by pose waits for the empty-robot drive test. `MERGE_HEIGHT` 100000 turns the merging off.

### First run (14:36 and 14:41 TeleOps, 25 ms thread)
The thread cost nothing measurable at 25 ms: the loop ran 25.8 / 25.6 ms mean (max 82 / 80) against the TeleOp's 25.9, 11 percent of the loops over 40 ms (8 percent without the thread; 17 percent at 30 ms and 26 at 20 ms), 1 to 2 Pinpoint blips per 120 to 170 s; each read 6.4 ms of wall-clock time, 26 percent of it, interval 25.0 ms with at most 28 ms. The 14:41 run (robot stationary, 11 salvos and 3 rejects with nothing in it, 121 s): 0 entries and the proximity 65 to 67 throughout, so the pusher arm, salvos and rejects do not trigger CS5. In the 14:36 run the intake went OUT at 4 entered three times in four (the counter holding 3 or 4 each time) and back IN at the next salvo; the fourth was the reject bug above.

## The action: FULL means the intake goes OUT (2026-11-14, user)
The counter on CS1 to CS4 does not work when the track is over-full, and with pollen only and the pusher at HOLD the track holds **4**. So in the always-IN `TeleOp - Competition` (`Mechanisms.setOuttakeWhenFullAllowed(true)`; the master switch `OUTTAKE_WHEN_FULL` and `ENTRY_CAPACITY` 4 are Dashboard values): the robot's count is **the number of CS5 entries since the track was last emptied**; when it reaches 4 the intake runs **OUT** (the screen shows `FULL: INTAKE OUT`, the log `ENTRY FULL: ...`), and a fifth element can no longer sit under CS5. The count restarts at 0 when **a salvo starts**, when **a reject finishes**, or when **the counter on CS1 to CS4 has read 0 for 3 s** (`ENTRY_ZERO_RESET_SEC`: false peaks cannot pile up into a phantom FULL); the intake then goes back IN. Peaks of elements leaving while the intake is OUT are not counted: an entry is judged by the time of its PEAK against the stretches the intake was OUT (plus 0.4 s after; an entry is confirmed up to 1.5 s after its peak, so judging by the time of the log line, as the first version did, counted 6 elements leaving during a reject as entries and sent the intake OUT for nothing, 14:36 TeleOp at 124.9 s; the log says `the intake was OUT at its peak: not counted`). A new entry also restarts the 3 s of the counter's zero (the first version reset the count the instant the first element entered, because the counter had not seen it yet: the first FULL of the 14:36 run fired at the fifth entry instead of the fourth). A sanity check: CS5 can only declare FULL while the counter holds at least 2 (`ENTRY_FULL_MIN_COUNTER`; 0 turns it off; ignored when the counter is not reading), so a handful of false peaks in an almost empty robot cannot send the intake OUT. Not changed: the autos and the Launch Tuners keep their own intake rules (the tuners are still always IN), the operator's left trigger (OUT) and the reject still work, and the pusher lock is unchanged. New columns `cs5_held` (the count) and `cs5_full` (0 or 1) in every log.
Replayed on the 14:16 TeleOp by hand: the count reaches 4 at each of the first three loads (the 4th entry is logged about 0.5 s after the element passed: the entry is confirmed 0.4 s + 0.1 s after its peak, so up to a half second of delay before the intake goes OUT, enough for a fifth element in a tight grouping: the merge delay `MERGE_GAP_SEC` is the thing to shorten if that matters), and the pile-up at the end would have sent the intake OUT at about 66.4 s with the counter at 2.

## The valley rule (EntryPipeline, 2026-11-14, from the 14:16 TeleOp): OFF by default (`VALLEY_MAX` 100000, the user's decision: the robot goes OUT when full, which removes the parked-element case, and tight groupings whose dips do not return to the empty level are expected; `VALLEY_MAX` 3 turns it on again)
The 14:16 TeleOp (3 salvos, the operator: 4 pollen in the bot at every salvo; driven EMPTY for the first 30 s; at the end a nectar and too many pollen picked up and the robot stopped loaded): **0 entries in the 30 s of empty driving** (1080 in of path, 1621 deg of turning, raw proximity 64 to 67 throughout; it was within 6 in of a wall ahead for only 0.2 s, so walls and flowers are still not tested), **exactly 4 entries in each of the three loads** (the first 12 entries are the 12 pollen), then 20 entries at the loaded end (a nectar and pollen parked in front of the sensor: the proximity sat 6 to 7 counts above empty with wiggles of +6 to +15 on top). A real element's bump comes back DOWN to the empty level before the next (the valley before each of the first 16 entries: -2 to +2 counts around the empty level; the study run: -1 to +3); the wiggles at the end had valleys +4 to +8. So a peak whose valley is more than `VALLEY_MAX` (3) counts above the empty level is REJECTED (counted in the `rejected` figure of the logs, not as an entry). The empty level is the median of the first `BASELINE_SEC` (keep the intake clear then); it can only be LOWERED afterwards (a quiet 2 s clearly lower), so an object parked in front of the sensor can never raise it. `tools/entry/run-check.cmd` replays the 14:16 proximity through the same `EntryPipeline` class: 18 entries (0 empty, 15 up to the loaded end, 3 at the end for the nectar and the pollen that arrived), 14 wiggles rejected; the study run is still 31. The cost: dense clumps whose dips stay more than 3 counts above empty are undercounted (the 10 ms study run: 47 of 60 raw peaks kept, against 52 shot by the counter's count).

## What it logs
- `CS5 ENTRY COUNTER started ...` (the settings and whether the proximity preset applied) and `CS5 ENTRY COUNTER baseline: ...` (the empty level and noise over the first second after the thread starts: **keep the intake clear for the first second of the run**).
- `CS5 ENTRY n: a peak of +h counts (prox p) at t s of the thread | <the counter's held text>` for every counted element.
- `CS5 ENTRY THREAD: ...` every 15 s: the real interval, the read time, the share of the hub, entries, errors, and `CS5 ENTRY COUNTER NOT ANSWERING` / `answering again` if it stops for 0.5 s.
- `SALVO started: ... | CS5 shadow count: n entries since the last salvo started (m in all)`: compare n with the "N held" of the same line (the counter's count).
- Sample columns `cs5_entries`, `cs5_prox` and **`cs5f`** (2026-11-14) in every log: `cs5f` is every raw proximity sample since the previous row as `prox@-ms` (ms BEFORE that row's own time, so `row t - ms` is the log-clock time of the sample), about 5 per 100 ms row at 20 ms. A merged entry's line lists its raw peaks (`MERGED n peaks: +26@15.51 ...`) and says how many ms before the line the peak was.
- the verbose TeleOp screen shows `CS5 entries (shadow, not used)`.

## Known limits (not yet tested)
Pollen only so far (nectar may reflect differently); floor-fed elements; a stuck element that dwells counts once (two on one plateau count as one); the signal depends on the sensor's mounting.
