# Idea queue

Ideas agreed to keep but not to build yet, newest decisions at the top of each list. Move an idea out of here when it is started. (Started 2026-11-04.)

## Queue (in order)

1. **Automatic ramp stow in the TeleOp** (added 2026-11-05). While the ramp is deployed, stow it by itself once the robot has driven a set distance away from the flower it was gathering at, so nobody has to remember the operator's Y. Ideas: remember the nearest flower when the ramp deploys, stow when the robot is farther than a Dashboard distance from its center (the old auto rule was 24 in from the flower center; the pose check that `Mechanisms.requestStow` / `stowPending` already does is the place to reuse), log it like the other COMMAND lines, and keep the operator's X (deploy) and Y (stow at once) as overrides. The distance is to be chosen; the ramp must not be stowed while still on the flower (the pollen are still coming in), so measure from the flower, not from where the robot was when it deployed.
2. **Shot journal.** The operator taps the result after each salvo (made or missed, with where it landed) so the log gives a hit rate per position, cell and distance, which is what the aim-depth idea and the per-spot trims need. The D-pad reports (landed left / right / low / high) already exist; "made" is the new one. **Waiting on the user's choice of operator controls** (the operator's free buttons: A, and the stick buttons; never Start or Back).
3. **Flower Dash** (low priority). One TeleOp button that runs the auto's flower routine: drive to the flower pre-position, deploy the ramp, ram, jostle until full, back off. Reuses the Hive Dash route and follower plumbing and the auto's `Jostle`.
4. **Use the spare seconds in the solo auto** (FIRST USE BUILT 2026-11-05: the final gather of the alliance-wall flower and the park in the hive-side corner, `AutonSolo.FINAL_FLOWER_ENABLED`, test row T16; whatever time is left after it is what remains of this idea) (bottom of the queue; the autos need refinement first). `Auton - Solo` finishes in about 24 s of the 30 s; the spare time could fetch more pollen if the field has any worth taking. Needs the field's element layout and the scoring rules.
5. **Jam resolution feature** (added 2026-11-05; the user's list gave only the name, so the behavior is still to be defined together). What exists today: a pusher move that cannot finish stops after `Pusher.MOVE_TIMEOUT_S` (3 s) and leaves the arm where it is, the operator's left bumper + B (reject) cancels a salvo and reverses, the pusher's and the intake's motor currents are logged (`pusher_amps`, `intake_amps`), and the operator can force the intake OUT (left trigger). Open: what counts as a jam (pusher stall, intake current, no movement), what the robot should do by itself (back the intake out, reverse and retry the pusher), and what the operator sees. 2026-11-06: the user wants the jam resolution to be AUTOMATIC (an auto jam resolve): the intake current watch (`intake_amps`, `INTAKE HIGH CURRENT`) is the likely trigger to build on.
6. **Look at the loop-time logs** (added 2026-11-06, the user's reminder). The loop profiler lines (`LOOP PROFILE`, `tools/logs/loop_profile.py`) are in the logs; go through them again: the average was 28 to 32 ms with about 20 percent of loops over 40 ms, the color sensor read (about 7 ms), the Pinpoint read and the pusher being the biggest items (design/hub-io notes). 2026-11-06: looked at, left alone on purpose (the user: "leave it all alone for now but keep an eye on any noteworthy changes"). Baseline to compare against: TeleOp 31.6 ms average (23 percent of loops over 40 ms; color sensors 7.9, other 6.6, Pinpoint 5.6, pusher 4.1), autos 22 ms; it was 27 to 29 ms on the evening of 11-05. If it grows or spikes past about 150 ms again, say so.
7. **Telemetry formatting** (added 2026-11-06). The user's nine pictures of the Telemetry Font Test arrived the same day and are written up in [telemetry-fonts.md](telemetry-fonts.md) (what works, what is ignored, how much fits: about 15 to 16 lines of about 36 characters, `<big>` and colors work, font-size attributes and backgrounds do not). BUILT 2026-11-06: the competition TeleOp's short HTML screen (`CompetitionScreen`, test row T21), the old screen behind `TELEMETRY_VERBOSE`; what is left is the check on the Driver Station and any change the user wants after seeing it.
